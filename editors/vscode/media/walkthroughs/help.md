# When something goes wrong

- **zfls: Show Output** shows the messages of zfls and of the extension,
  such as why a compiler, a formatter or a debugger could not run. With
  **Developer: Set Log Level...** at Debug, it also shows the command lines
  they ran with and what they printed.
- **zfls: Restart Language Server** starts zfls again, after it has stopped
  or when its index looks out of date.
- **zfls: Show Module Compile Order** lists the order in which the workspace's
  modules must be compiled.

Questions and problems are welcome at
[github.com/cesiumlang/zfls/issues](https://github.com/cesiumlang/zfls/issues).
